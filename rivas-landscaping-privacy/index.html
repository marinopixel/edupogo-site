Rivas Landscaping Privacy Policy

Edupogo LLC · Effective November 25, 2026

Information on your device

Rivas Landscaping stores jobs, notes, optional customer names, addresses and phone numbers, selected photos, revisions and preferences on your device. It has no app account, ads, in-app analytics or automatic company sync. Selected photos may contain camera or location metadata. The app does not request live location or access your address book.

Directions, sharing and backups

Directions pass the address you enter to your chosen map service. Reports, printing, file selection and sharing use the destinations you choose. Those services handle their copies under their own policies. Reports and unencrypted manual backups may contain customer information and original photos. Keep backups in a private location outside the original device if you need to restore them later. Preparing an export may leave a temporary copy until device cleanup.

Retention and deletion

Job records remain until you delete them. Deleting a job removes its saved history and photos used only by that job. Prior backups, reports and other copies may remain where they were saved. An old backup may restore a deleted job. Uninstalling or clearing app storage may remove local records. Edupogo LLC cannot remotely access or delete your local records.

Support and privacy requests

Email marino@edupogo.com to contact Edupogo LLC for support or to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, where applicable. We may need to verify your request. Support receives your email address, message and files you choose to send and uses them to respond. Google Workspace processes our support email and may process it outside your country. We keep correspondence as needed to respond and resolve the issue and for applicable legal obligations. Start with the app version and a description using fictional examples or redacted screenshots. Avoid sending backups, original customer photos, passwords or unrelated information.

Google Play and this website

Google Play handles purchases under its own privacy terms and may provide Android vitals reports about eligible devices. GitHub Pages hosts this notice and may log visitor IP addresses for security. This page contains no advertising, analytics or tracking scripts.
