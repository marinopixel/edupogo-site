PogoDart mobile support

PogoDart teaches Dart and Flutter by doing. It is operated by Edupogo LLC.

Start learning

  1. Open Learn and tap Start lesson.
  2. Read the short explanation. Tap an underlined word for its meaning.
  3. Type code in the editor and tap Run. Read the result or the explanation of what to fix.
  4. Finish the exercises and play all five game levels to open the next lesson.

Restore a purchase

Use the Apple or Google store account that made the purchase. Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Connect to the internet for the restore. Apple, Google and website purchases are separate.

Progress and notes

Your progress and notes stay on your device. They do not sync between devices or with the website. Changing or removing devices may lose progress unless your device backup restores it. A certificate PDF is not a backup of your learning data.

Get help

Email support@edupogo.com. Include PogoDart, your device model, app version and what happened. If comfortable, attach a screenshot without private information. Never send your password or full payment card number.
