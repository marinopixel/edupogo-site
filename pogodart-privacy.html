PogoDart mobile privacy

Effective date: November 25, 2026.

PogoDart is operated by Edupogo LLC. This notice describes the Android, iPhone and iPad learning app. The website has separate services and account handling.

Your learning data

Lesson progress, game results, review cards, notes and preferences are saved on your device. Your code runs on the device. The app does not upload your code or explanations to a teaching server. Your progress does not sync with the website or other devices.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment. RevenueCat helps the app check and restore your purchase. Purchase records and an app user identifier are processed for these functions and RevenueCat purchase reporting. Edupogo does not receive your payment card details through the app. Store and RevenueCat services may process technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot purchases.

The app does not offer learner sign-in in this release. Purchases can be restored through the same store account. A purchase on Apple, Google or the website does not automatically unlock the other platforms.

Sharing and support

If you export a completion certificate, you choose its name and where to share it through your device. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not include payment card details in support requests.

Advertising and tracking

PogoDart has no advertisements or advertising tracking. We do not ask the app to collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings.

Retention and your choices

You can edit or clear your explanations in the lessons. Device backups may contain local app data according to your device settings. Removing the app and any device backups removes your local copies; device behavior varies. This does not refund a purchase or remove records held by Apple, Google or RevenueCat. Contact us about purchase-related data or support correspondence. Some records may be retained to meet legal obligations.

Request deletion of purchase-related data

Email support@edupogo.com with the subject “PogoDart data deletion”. Tell us whether you use Android or Apple. We may ask for a store order reference to locate and verify the purchase-related record. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers or identity documents.

We will review the request and explain what can be deleted and any records that must be retained for legal, accounting or fraud-prevention reasons. Removing purchase records may affect our ability to restore access; it does not cancel or refund a store purchase. Apple and Google handle requests about their own records separately.

Contact

Email support@edupogo.com and include “PogoDart” in your message.
