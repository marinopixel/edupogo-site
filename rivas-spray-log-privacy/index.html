Rivas Spray Log Privacy Policy

Edupogo LLC · Effective November 24, 2026

Records in the app

Rivas Spray Log stores the information you enter on your device, including farm and field details, application records, names, corrections, notes and imported labels. The app requires no user account and includes no ads or in-app analytics. Edupogo LLC does not automatically receive your records or provide automatic cloud backup in this version.

Exports, sharing and backups

You choose where to save, print or share reports and backups. Files may contain names, location details and attachments. The destination you choose handles its copy under its own policies. Rivas Spray Log does not encrypt exported backups. Your operating system may also back up app data according to your device settings. Preparing an export can leave a temporary file until device storage cleanup.

Retention and deletion

Corrections preserve earlier versions, and archiving a field preserves its history. Saved records do not expire automatically. You can discard unfinished drafts. Uninstalling or clearing app storage can remove local records. Manage exported copies or cloud backups where you saved them. Edupogo LLC cannot remotely access or delete your local records.

Support

If you email support, Edupogo LLC receives your email address, displayed name and the message and files you choose to send. We use these to respond and investigate problems. Google Workspace processes and stores our support email and may process it outside your country, including in the United States. We keep support correspondence for 12 calendar months after the issue closes, subject to documented holds and provider recovery periods. Send only what is needed, and leave out passwords and unrelated records.

Google Play and this website

Google Play handles purchases under its own privacy terms. Google may provide Android vitals reports about crashes and performance from eligible devices, subject to users’ device settings. GitHub Pages hosts this notice and may log visitor IP addresses for security. This page contains no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Your privacy choices

Contact Edupogo LLC at rivas-support@edupogo.com for questions or to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, where applicable. We may need to verify your request. Please contact us if a child has sent personal information to support so we can address it.
