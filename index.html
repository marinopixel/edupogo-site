Built for children who learn differently

Dyslexia, ADHD, autism, reading and speech difficulties. You tell Pogo at setup and he changes how he explains things — shorter sentences, literal language, more structure, whatever fits.

  • Pogo reads aloud

    Every answer can be spoken, for children who find reading hard going.

  • Ask by speaking

    Children who struggle to type can just say the question out loud.

  • Text that grows

    Everything scales with the system text size, right up to 200%.

  • VoiceOver and Voice Control

    Every button labelled and reachable, in all eight languages.

  • Reduced motion

    Turn on Reduce Motion and the animations stop.

  • High contrast

    Colour is never the only thing telling you what something means.

Eight languages, all the way down

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese — the whole app, not just the menus. Pogo answers in whichever language your child chooses.

Safe by design

  • No ads, ever

    Nothing is sold to your child, and nothing is sold about them.

  • Filtered coming and going

    Questions are checked before Pogo sees them, and answers before your child does.

  • Hard questions go to you

    Violence, death and grown-up topics are handed back to a parent, warmly and without drama.

  • Nothing is kept

    No accounts, no conversation logs. Your child's profile never leaves their device.

  • Daily limits built in

    Edupogo is meant to be a tool, not somewhere to disappear into.

  • A grown-up sets it up

    An adult creates the profile and chooses what fits their child.
