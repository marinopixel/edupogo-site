Rivas Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026.

Who operates the app

Rivas Calculator is operated by Edupogo LLC. This policy covers Rivas Calculator.

Calculations and preferences

Calculator processes calculations on your device. It has no app account, advertising, analytics, in-app billing or feature that uploads calculations to Edupogo LLC.

Theme, selected subject, Workbook choice, Haptics choice, theme-picker position, regional choice and preferred geometry unit are stored locally. A separate local store saves the last three completed calculations across all six subjects, including entered values, original unit or angle context where applicable, and displayed answers. These read-only snapshots remain after restarting until cleared or replaced by newer completions. Uncompleted input fields are not restored.

Backups and deletion

The app has no cloud synchronization service. Your operating system may handle backups, transfers and storage separately, including preferences and saved history. Removing local app data may leave existing backup copies.

Open the header History button (clock-arrow icon) and choose Clear History to remove saved history from local app storage. This does not clear current inputs or existing platform backups. If storage fails, the history view shows a warning and lets you retry. Fully quitting clears unfinished inputs and retains saved history. Switching away may keep the session running. You can remove saved preferences and history through platform app-data controls. There is no app account to delete.

Support

Contact support@edupogo.com with support or privacy questions, including requests about information you sent us.

Messages may include your email address, message text and files you choose to send. Please avoid passwords and unrelated sensitive details. We use these messages to respond and resolve the issue. We retain messages and attachments for 12 months after the issue closes, subject to documented holds. Reopening starts a new period when the issue closes again. Calculator cases have a separate private business register with case references and review dates. Files remain in business Gmail. Minimal cleanup records contain only case identifiers, dates and actions and are kept for 12 months after cleanup, subject to documented holds. Retention is reviewed manually. Provider backups and recovery systems may retain copies.

Store services

Paid downloads are processed by the store where you buy the app, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store. Stores and operating systems may process purchases, backups and diagnostics under their own services. Calculator does not send calculations to a business diagnostic service. Store-provided reports are separate from the app.

Service providers and website

The app’s privacy link opens a fixed address containing no calculations or saved history. The website host receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address. Opening the link is optional. Calculation features work without loading the website.

We use Google Workspace for correspondence you choose to send. Google’s Cloud Data Processing Addendum covers customer content, and its Cloud Privacy Notice explains service and operational information. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. These providers may process information outside your country. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Device-backup providers have their own practices. Calculator has no feature that automatically uploads local calculations to these providers.

Younger users and future changes

Calculator is intended for ages 9 and up, including older children, teens and adults. Some themes include mild fantasy/action artwork. Each store assigns its rating from the final content declarations. Younger users should ask a parent or trusted adult for help before sending personal information to support.

Adding accounts, synchronization, analytics or other services would require a fresh privacy review and updated information. Calculator currently has none of these services.
